About me

Designer, developer, and obsessive craftsperson.

I’m Abrar — a designer who codes and a developer who designs. I specialize in building premium, motion-rich digital experiences that feel intentional and alive.

My work lives at the intersection of interface design, animation systems, and real engineering. I’ve shipped everything from cinematic web experiences and 3D product tools to design systems and interactive storytelling pieces.

WHAT I CARE ABOUT
Motion as meaning
Animation isn’t decoration — it’s how you guide attention, create emotion, and make interfaces feel human.
Craft at every scale
From a single hover state to an entire product system, the details are where trust is built.
Performance as respect
Beautiful work that feels slow is a betrayal. I obsess over speed, load times, and perceived performance.
Clarity over novelty
New technology is only useful when it serves the person on the other side of the screen.
